<p><b>Pets</b> are a feature added to <i>Grow a Garden</i> on <a href="/wiki/Update_Log/1.04.0" title="Update Log/1.04.0">Update 1.04.0</a> (May 3, 2025).
</p><p>Pets possess unique abilities, having multiple of the same pets can stack their abilities. Players start with <b>3</b> equip slots, but can get an additional <b>5</b> slots from buying gamepasses from the <a href="/wiki/Limited_Time_Shop" title="Limited Time Shop">Limited Time Shop</a> and/or trading aged pets in. 
</p><p>Players can hold a maximum of <b>60</b> pets in their <a href="/wiki/Inventory" title="Inventory">inventory</a>. As of the <a href="/wiki/Christmas_Harvest_Event" title="Christmas Harvest Event">Christmas Harvest</a> Update, players can increase it by buying game passes in the <a href="/wiki/Limited_Time_Shop" title="Limited Time Shop">Limited Time Shop</a>, trading pets in, Crafting a <a href="/wiki/Pet_Pouch" title="Pet Pouch">Pet Pouch</a>, or buying space from the <a href="/wiki/Garden_Ascension" title="Garden Ascension">Garden Ascension</a> Shop, with a maximum of <b>285</b> pets total.
